IFFL Privacy Policy

Insanity Fantasy Football League · Last updated: June 12, 2026

IFFL ("the app") is a private application for members of the Insanity Fantasy Football League. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Information We Collect

  • Account information — your name and email address, provided when you sign in with Apple, Google, or email/password.
  • League activity — your fantasy team assignment, roster moves, trade proposals and responses, and player trade-interest signals you create in the app.
  • Profile preferences — an optional nickname, team logo, and display settings you choose.
  • Push notification token — a device identifier used solely to deliver trade notifications to your device.

How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and grant access to your league.
  • To display rosters, trades, and league activity to members of your league.
  • To send you notifications about trades that involve your team.
  • To save your personal app preferences.

How Your Information Is Stored

Account and league data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Messaging). Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and is accessible only to authenticated members of the league. Firebase's handling of data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Information Sharing

Your league activity (team, roster, and trades) is visible to other members of your private league, as is necessary for the app to function. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing.

Sign in with Apple

If you sign in with Apple, you may choose to hide your email address. The app works fully with a private relay email and never requires you to reveal your real address.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained while you are an active member of the league. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us at the email below and your information will be removed from the league database.

Children's Privacy

IFFL is intended for league members aged 17 and older and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

Contact
